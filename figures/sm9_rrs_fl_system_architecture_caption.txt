System architecture of SM9-RRS-FL. (1) Setup: the D-KGC provisions client private keys. (2) Federated learning: clients upload signed local updates, while the aggregation server (AS) distributes the global model. (3) Poisoning detection: the AS evaluates client updates and adjusts their aggregation contributions. (4) Trace and revocation: qualifying anomalies trigger evidence submission to the auditor, followed by threshold tracing through the D-KGC. The AS permanently revokes an identified client only after verifying the tracing result, task-label consistency, and the threshold certificate.
